# Economy Design v1.0

## Principles
- Jobs create money.
- PvP redistributes money.
- Progression is controlled through configurable values.
- No XP or player levels.

## Starting Values
- Starting Cash: 1000
- Max Energy: 10
- Energy Regen: 1 every 7 minutes
- Bank Deposit Fee (future): 15%

## PvP Economy
- Successful attack only.
- Maximum stealable cash = Victim Unprotected Cash × 5%
- Battle Rating = 0.5 (Alpha)
- Final Cash Won = Maximum Stealable Cash × Battle Rating

## Future Modifiers
- Attack Items
- Defensive Items
- Clubs
- Events
- Boosts

All values must be configurable and never hardcoded in business logic.
